# Exact equivalent partial-state merging: M4A1 research

2026-11-18. Research only: no implementation, policy change, upstream-repository investigation or heavy benchmark. Scope: M4A1 `18010000001`, Detonation, Lowest Recoil, 800 points, Insight 3/7 `13110000091` locked at root optic `11`. The opt-in dominance domain is **1,696,710 ordinary layouts**; getting strictly below one million needs at least 696,711 fewer represented scoring states (41.06%). Exact compression may reduce represented states and work while preserving all paths; it does **not** make those original concrete layouts cease to exist. No sub-million result is established here. [Current measured profile](archive/m4a1_dominance_prototype.md); [earlier alternatives](archive/m4a1_layout_reduction_options.md).

## Finding

Build a small **layered exact decision diagram** over closed ordinary attachment groups before considering a general solver rewrite. Merge prefixes only when every common future continuation has identical legality and observable behavior. Keep incoming attachment-choice arcs/backpointers, so multiple layouts share one suffix expansion without losing their identities. This is the exact-state DP construction described by van Hoeve, §2; arbitrary width-limiting merges instead produce relaxations and cannot be treated as exact enumeration. Exact diagrams can still be exponential. [Author's tutorial](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/vanhoeve/papers/DD_TutORial.pdf).

The most promising *unverified* opportunity is **compensating effects across several ordinary leaf choices**: two different rear-grip/stock/foregrip or accessory prefixes could reach the same exact native fold, cost and continuation interface even though no individual parts are equivalent. Existing full-effect leaf grouping found only 0.044% additional reduction, so repeating per-item grouping is unlikely to meet the target. There is no collision census yet showing that compound equivalence is common after dominance. [Measured grouping result](archive/m4a1_layout_reduction_options.md); [choice-group implementation](../tools/choice_groups.mjs).

## Necessary equivalence contract

For prefixes A and B at one decision layer, require a continuation-preserving correspondence: the same remaining choice labels are legal, and applying any suffix gives the same native objective, requested stats, residual budget behavior and downstream tactical/calibration possibilities. This is a sufficient implementation contract rather than a claim to have derived the minimal state. Keeping too much state loses compression; omitting a live dependency loses correctness. Hooker's weighted-DD formulation makes the distinction between identical feasibility subgraphs and identical objective functions explicit. [Hooker, §§3–4, author's paper](https://johnhooker.tepper.cmu.edu/DDandDP2.pdf).

Use a request-local key with the following components:

1. **Layer and remaining legality.** Include the next layer and canonical remaining domains, plus the complete reduced constraints over remaining decisions. Reuse/refactor the conceptual `residualKey(depth)` from the counter: it reduces Boolean auxiliaries, subtracts fixed contributions, combines coefficients and drops tautologies. Equal low/high constraint intervals are insufficient. The counter's empty key means an unconstrained suffix *count*; it does not mean all suffixes have the same score. Different request restrictions, dominance rules or layer orders must not share a key. [mount_search.mjs](../tools/mount_search.mjs).

2. **Mounted boundary and replacement behavior.** Preserve any selected/default/bundled provider still read by future rules; active host/socket availability, child slots, occupancy, coupling conditions, explicit-purchase retention, locks and bound-ammunition state. `compileMounts()` symbolically unrolls a mounting fixed point: an explicit purchase is not simply an additive mounted part, and future selections can suppress defaults through occupancy. A prefix cannot be permanently folded until all future choices that could replace its mounted effects are closed. Start with leaves whose parents and replacement rules are already fixed. Full residual legality preserves feasible suffixes but does not alone preserve the mounted-effect contributions of those suffixes. [scip_mount_model.mjs](../tools/scip_mount_model.mjs).

3. **Exact resumable native numeric state and reference state.** Include all live accumulator bucket fields, main/display attributes and source reference replacements, not rounded Control/Handling/Stability or a final score. Initially retain the exact ordered reference-operation tokens. Preserve IEEE-754 values, signed zero and value types in serialization; reject nonfinite or unsupported states. Do not sort floating-point additions or replace sequential folds with algebraic sums. An exact folded state can replace its history only if native continuation consumes that state alone; otherwise retain the ordered operation stream as a conservative fallback. The existing evaluator exposes `numeric`, `references`, `targets`, `mapped` and `selectOperations`; it clones buckets and resumes the actual native fold rather than inventing a new formula. [shared_stats.mjs](../tools/shared_stats.mjs); [pinned accessor installation](../tools/prepare_project_runtime.py).

4. **Selection and identity dependencies outside numeric buckets.** Retain source-ordered tactical-effective candidate information until strongest-per-target selection is settled; folding a provisional winner and then simply adding a stronger later device is wrong. Include the selected references/profiles and any part-identity information read by fire-control logic. The timed trajectory independently calls `calculate()` and resolves fire control using `state.appliedParts`; equality of the shared-stat core alone does not establish equality of timed recoil. The preset freezes chart eligibility per run and ranks a 20-point trajectory, speed-tier penalty, camera/kick and Handling. [laser_effects.mjs](../tools/laser_effects.mjs); [timed_recoil.mjs](../tools/timed_recoil.mjs); [optimizer_presets.mjs](../tools/optimizer_presets.mjs).

5. **Exact cost / resource state.** Start with spent cost in the key, with exact budget policy. Same default effects at different costs can admit different suffixes and tactical completions; cost also affects selected output categories. Keeping only the cheaper prefix is dominance, not identity-preserving equivalence. Even if the residual attachment-budget row encodes cost, retain explicit cost for reconstruction/ranking unless redundancy is proved. [scip_mount_model.mjs](../tools/scip_mount_model.mjs); [optimizer_policy.mjs](../tools/optimizer_policy.mjs).

6. **Tactical continuation interface.** Include available concrete rail/socket paths, compatible effect families and costs, shared occupancy, locked/default devices, enabled-laser allowance and parent-provided child sockets. A scalar number of free rails is insufficient: equal capacities can have different matchings. The existing tactical planner uses per-slot choices and explicit matching, including laser replacement and patch capacity. Canonicalize interchangeable sockets only with a reconstructable bijection respecting locks, costs and attachment effects. [choice_groups.mjs](../tools/choice_groups.mjs).

7. **Tuning continuation interface.** For calibrated equivalence, retain occurrence paths, legal notch/step positions, tune functions/targets/modifiers and their binding to source operations. Equal default effects do not imply equal reachable tuning vectors. A set of independently nondominated per-part tuning points also need not preserve the jointly reachable, correlated build vectors. Initially keep distinct scored-tuning identities; later merge only with a certified correspondence between complete reachable tuning states. Existing grouping deliberately separates scored calibration. [choice_groups.mjs](../tools/choice_groups.mjs); [dominance_reduction.mjs](../tools/dominance_reduction.mjs).

Request-wide mode, source/data hashes, scorer functions/weights, firing mode, floors, lock set and objective contract should namespace the table. Fail closed to unmerged traversal for unknown contracts. Objective-target filtering can reduce keys later, but requires proving that omitted targets never feed native mappings, source references, special fire control, floor checks or future tuning. Existing dominance's scoped exclusions are not automatically a general equivalence certificate. [dominance_reduction.mjs](../tools/dominance_reduction.mjs); [shared_stats.mjs](../tools/shared_stats.mjs).

## Reconstruction, multiplicity and the original 5% gate

Preserve **incoming arcs**, each labelled with the actual path/item purchase, plus BigInt path multiplicity. A merged node shares future transitions; it must not select an arbitrary prefix and discard the others. Reconstruct lazily from the DAG. Independent alias sets per socket are unsafe when only some combinations actually appeared: the calibration model explicitly fixes one complete survivor row to prevent cross-mixing. [scip_mount_model.mjs](../tools/scip_mount_model.mjs).

For the original grouped baseline, N = 12,885,654 and K = ceil(N/20) = **644,283**. Rank weighted terminal states against that original denominator, then expand exactly the required original layouts. Preserve actual comparison tolerance, cost rules and deterministic tie order at a cutoff; do not invent one tie winner per equivalence class. A terminal class can straddle K, so retain its concrete rank order or an equivalent deterministic reconstruction scheme. [Baseline contract](archive/m4a1_exhaustive_screening_feasibility.md); [comparison semantics](../tools/optimizer_policy.mjs).

The dominance-reduced population cannot recover that original gate merely by assigning an integer weight to each retained winner: dominated removed layouts generally had different scores, costs or continuations. To certify the original gate, run exact alias-preserving compression on the **baseline** domain or separately reconstruct and score discarded populations with a valid cutoff proof. The current study explicitly records only partition best previews, not legacy survivor storage. [Dominance guarantee limits](archive/m4a1_dominance_prototype.md); [exhaustive_screening_study.mjs](../tools/exhaustive_screening_study.mjs).

A default-screening equivalence can still save scoring when aliases have different tuning: propagate the winning ordinary score to all qualifying aliases, then calibrate their distinct domains. Calibrating only one representative is valid only after tactical and tuning equivalence are separately established. Even preserving the original staged screen does not prove a global calibrated optimum, because that screen already excludes lower-ranked ordinary defaults. [Staged contract](exhaustive_staged_screening.md); [tuning safeguards](../tools/choice_groups.mjs).

## Proposed bounded experiment

1. **Measure collision potential before implementation.** Freeze this request and source hashes. On reduced-budget fixtures, instrument a layer-wise closed-leaf prototype with a hierarchy of keys: residual legality only; plus exact numeric/reference state; plus cost; plus tactical interface; plus tuning interface. The loose keys diagnose where collisions disappear and must never authorize pruning. Report unique states, merged incoming paths and weighted terminal counts at every layer.
2. **Try small ordinary blocks.** Hold barrel and other structural providers fixed; examine rear-grip/stock/foregrip and ordinary leaf accessories where source rules permit. Test several legal layer orders without changing native folding order. The current 102 barrel/stock partitions keep workers disjoint but prevent sharing stock alternatives across partitions: a stock-inclusive block needs a changed partition plan, a local precomputed block table, or a second experiment explicitly measuring the lost cross-partition sharing. [Study partitioning](../tools/exhaustive_screening_study.mjs).
3. **Validate the transition contract.** Exhaust all suffixes of tiny fixtures and compare merged/unmerged legality, complete mounted identities, exact unrounded native states, objective vectors, cost and reconstructable layout multiset. Include equal displayed-stat counterexamples, compensating operations, bundled replacement, reference overrides, laser ties, locks/descendants, bound ammo, unsafe numeric policies and scored tuning. Test cache saturation/cancellation with exact fallback.
4. **Keep guarantee levels separate.** First certify default ordinary scoring; then tactical continuation; then calibration correspondence. Check original weighted K survivors and tie-boundary identities on small baseline domains. Existing source/tuning replay tests are useful templates, not a proof for a new merge key.
5. **Only then measure the full fixture.** Compare baseline and dominance profiles independently. Record represented layouts, weighted original layouts, scored terminal classes, expanded DP nodes, key-building time, cache memory, reconstruction/survivor memory and complete wall time. Require uncached native replay of reconstructed winners and full count reconciliation. Distinguish a sub-million terminal-class result from sub-million DP nodes or source calls.

Expected risks are expensive canonical serialization, wide exact state tables, missed collisions from over-specific keys, and unsound correlations lost during reconstruction. Bound memory by falling back to ordinary traversal or evicting reusable memo entries; never force unlike states together merely to hit a width or count target. Decision-diagram branch-and-bound caching can later combine equivalence with proved dominance/suboptimality thresholds, but that is a separate pruning proof, not permission to collapse unequal states. [Author tutorial, exact/relaxed distinction](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/vanhoeve/papers/DD_TutORial.pdf); [primary caching paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.13118).

**Recommendation:** implement the collision census and a small closed-leaf exact DAG first. Compound effect cancellation is the strongest plausible new merging opportunity; tactical count symmetry is already substantially exploited. No evidence currently supports promising the additional 41.06% reduction, and conservative calibrated identity requirements may leave little useful compression.
